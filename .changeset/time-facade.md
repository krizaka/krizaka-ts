New package: date arithmetic on an instant (`addSeconds`, `addMinutes`, `addHours`, `subSeconds`, `subMinutes`, `subHours`, `differenceInSeconds`, `differenceInMilliseconds`, `isBefore`, `isAfter`), exact in any time zone: the facade the « Krizaka first » dates rule points to (krizaka-ts#76), so the new packages import no date library of their own.
