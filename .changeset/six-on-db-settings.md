`settingsSchema` and `pricesSchema`: the settings and price tables as `@krizaka/db` modules, version 1 byte-identical to `settingsMigration` and `pricesMigration` (a product adopts with no DDL change). `Query` is `@krizaka/db`'s, re-exported under the same name. The two migration functions are deprecated (#51).
